## What is Microteia?

Microteia is an **open-source platform for building business software on Firebase**.

In most business systems, adding a single field means a developer, a migration, a review and a deploy, while the client waits. Microteia flips that around: you write a business rule the way your client would say it, and Microteia turns it into a working application, installed in a Firebase project **you own**.

## How it works

Every sentence you write is broken down into pieces the platform already knows how to run:

| Piece | What it becomes |
|---|---|
| **Screens** | Designed, not coded |
| **Fields & option lists** | The data model of your business |
| **Buttons & automations** | Readable workflows that act on your records |
| **Permissions** | Who can see and do what |
| **Client portal** | A place for your clients to follow their own data |

Each piece is a **declaration**, not custom code, so the platform can run it, check it and evolve it safely.

## Why Microteia

- **Starts with a sentence.** Describe the rule in VS Code, together with your AI coding assistant (e.g. Claude Code via MCP).
- **Verifiable before it ships.** Anything that doesn't fit is refused on your machine, with the reason, in seconds, not in production.
- **Extensions per organization.** Business logic lives in extensions installed per organization. The kernel knows nothing about any business.
- **Five layers, one is yours.** Of the platform's five layers, you only write the extensions.
- **Production-proven.** Microteia already runs a law firm in production, with 278 releases and 515 test files.
- **Open source.** No license fee, no per-user pricing. You only pay for Firebase.
